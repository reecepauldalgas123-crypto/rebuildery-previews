Kitchens

Kitchens to suit every budget

From traditional styles with doors and drawers set inside timber frames and timber drawer boxes, to a more economical range with face-mounted doors and metal drawer boxes. Choose a simple MDF kitchen for a paint finish or solid timber such as oak, maple or ash. Our spraying service can match and mix colours to suit you.

  • In-frame kitchens
  • Solid oak, maple and ash
  • Painted MDF
  • Colour-matched spraying
