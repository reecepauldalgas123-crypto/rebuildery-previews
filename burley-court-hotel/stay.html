Lounge and bar

Light and airy, and ideal for refreshments or a light lunch from our bar snack menu, served daily from 12 until 2pm.

Children

Children aged 14 and under stay free when sharing a room with two adults, with meals charged. Under 3s are not able to join us in the restaurant at night; high tea can be served in the room or TV room.

Dogs

Dogs are welcome in the hotel, excluding the public rooms, at £10 a day, with owners responsible for any damage.

Meals for B&B guests

Breakfast £11 and dinner £20. Meals served in your bedroom carry a £2.50 service charge.
