Our story

From Leigh Road to three surgeries

Some of our team have been with us for decades. Here is how we got here.

  1. 1958

    Mr Neal and Mr Davies found a mixed practice in Leigh Road, Eastleigh. It later becomes widely known as Davies and Aylott and moves to small animals only.

  2. 1991

    Leslie Montgomery joins as an assistant and starts a career here spanning three decades.

  3. 1997

    Clare, once the practice's Saturday girl, returns as a vet after Bristol Vet School, with her husband Beyers van Niekerk.

  4. 2000

    Beyers becomes a partner.

  5. 2016

    Clare and Beyers become full owners of the practice.

  6. 2025

    Village Vets in Pilgrims Close joins the family, alongside Chandlers Ford and Boyatt Wood.
