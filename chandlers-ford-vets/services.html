Common questions

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Consultations are by appointment only, so please call your surgery to book.

What happens in an emergency?

In opening hours, call your surgery immediately and tell us it is urgent so we can see you as a priority. Out of hours, call Vets Now on 01962 808306. Vets Now fees apply in the evenings and at weekends.

Are you taking new clients?

We run a waiting list and prioritise pets who are not registered with any vet, such as puppies, kittens and pets who have just moved to the area. You can join the list online.

Can you see exotic pets?

We see cats, dogs and small pets. For particularly difficult cases or exotic species, we may refer you to a vet who specialises in that field.
