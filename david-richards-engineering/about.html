Our story

Experts in ultra hard cutting tool technology.

Using the latest CNC grinding and spark erosion techniques, we manufacture a wide range of standard PCBN and PCD tools to stock. Over many years we have been involved in many new and challenging projects, and that experience is there for every customer who calls.
