Grades

PCBN and PCD grades for every cut.

Low CBN content grades keep heat at the cutting point for wear resistance in finishing. High CBN grades, bonded to tungsten carbide or in solid indexable form, take on tougher cuts. Our diamond grades handle abrasive non-ferrous work.

  • Low CBN: DR-50, DR-450 and DR-500
  • High CBN: DR-75, 85, 95, 400, 650 and 900
  • Solid high CBN: DR-100
  • Diamond: DR-PCD and multimodal MM DR-PCD
