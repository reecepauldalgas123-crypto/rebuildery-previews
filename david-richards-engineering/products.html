PCBN

Machining hard steel

PCBN tools machine hardened steels with apparent ease. At relatively high surface speeds, heat generated at the cut softens the material locally, and the heat is carried away in the swarf. Low CBN grades are best for light finishing cuts; high CBN grades take on heavier work.

  • Hard turning
  • Finish cutting
  • Low and high CBN grades
  • Solid DR-100 inserts

PCBN

Machining cast iron

Cast iron is not generally very hard, but it is abrasive. PCBN gives a cost effective and highly productive alternative to hard metal or ceramic cutting tools, thanks to its resistance to abrasion.

  • Grey and chilled irons
  • Rough and finish machining
  • Longer tool life

Diamond

Polycrystalline diamond (PCD)

DR-PCD tools last longer, cut faster and can replace grinding with milling and turning. Our ISO standard PCD inserts suit silicon aluminium alloys, copper, brass and abrasive plastics, with other geometries for MMC and very high silicon alloys, many ex-stock.

  • DR-PCD (10 micron)
  • MM DR-PCD multimodal
  • Fixed pocket cutters
  • Precision PCD inserts
