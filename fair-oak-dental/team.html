Dr Namrata Vira

Owner and principal dentist

Namrata graduated in Mumbai in 2003 and passed her UK exams in 2010. She has a special interest in facial aesthetics, holds a certificate from the Royal College of Surgeons, London, and is a certified Invisalign provider. She loves creating beautiful smiles through straightening, composite bonding and veneers, and particularly enjoys helping nervous patients relax.
