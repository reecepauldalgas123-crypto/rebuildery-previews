Preventive package

Problems caught early can be treated with minimal damage to your natural teeth. Our preventive package keeps you one step ahead.

  • Tooth decay screening
  • Advice on preventing decay
  • Oral hygiene coaching
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gum disease screening
  • Hygienist appointments

Aesthetic

Smile makeovers

Both principal dentists are certified Invisalign providers and love creating beautiful, natural smiles with the least invasive approach.

  • Invisalign clear aligners
  • Teeth whitening
  • Composite bonding
  • Veneers
  • Smile recontouring and design

Implants

Dental implants

Implants are titanium roots that bond naturally with the bone. Crowns, bridges and dentures can then be fitted on to them, and they can make full dentures far more secure. Dr Heril Haria has a keen interest in implant dentistry.

  • Single tooth implants
  • Implant bridges
  • Implant-retained dentures
  • Bone grafting

Surgical

Oral surgery

Wisdom tooth removal and other minor surgical procedures, carried out in the practice.

Sedation

IV sedation

If you are very anxious about treatment, intravenous sedation helps you feel relaxed while staying conscious. Ask your dentist or any of the team for more information.
