About us

Serving Solent boat owners for over 50 years.

Two boatyards on the River Hamble, an engineering workshop and a well stocked parts shop, all run by people who know boats.

Our story

Built on Volvo Penta know-how.

R.K. Marine began as a marine engineering business and has worked with Volvo Penta engines and drives for over 50 years. Over that time we built an outstanding boatyard for motor boats at Swanwick, and in the last 15 years added a yard for yachts at Warsash.

Today we service, inspect, repair and recondition engines, look after boats ashore and afloat, and supply engines and parts across the Solent and beyond.
