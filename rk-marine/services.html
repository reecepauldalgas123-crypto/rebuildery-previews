Berthing

Berths at Swanwick on the Hamble

Quiet, secure berths with quick access to the Solent by water and to the M27 at Junction 8 by road. Access is through a coded gate, and private berth holders have their own toilet and shower facilities with plenty of car parking.

  • 45 deep water berths
  • Motor boats up to 60 foot
  • Private, permanent and temporary berths
  • Electricity and water
  • Toilets and showers
  • Hard standing
