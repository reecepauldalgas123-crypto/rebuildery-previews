Our story

Over 50 years of blinds.

Established in 1967, Solaris Blinds have built up an esteemed reputation supplying and installing all types of blinds for domestic and commercial customers in and around Southampton and Portsmouth.

We believe in giving the best possible customer service, so each customer receives individual, friendly attention. Our experienced team are always on hand to give advice on the right blinds for each room and each purpose.
