Our story

Three generations of the Worley family.

Albert Edward Worley opened an antique shop and auction house in Purbrook, Hampshire in the late 1950s. With his son Adrian he formed A. E. Worley and Son Ltd in 1965.

In 1976 Adrian bought No. 76 Northam Road, Southampton, later adding No. 74. Since 2002 his sons James and Graham, both furniture makers, restorers, French polishers and upholsterers, have run the business.
