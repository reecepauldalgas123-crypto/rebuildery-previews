Stairs

Metal staircases and fire escapes

We have been designing, fabricating and installing steel stairs and fire escapes for more than twenty years. You will find our work in schools, factories, apartment blocks, shops and homes throughout the local area and beyond.

  • Fire escapes for first and second floor flats
  • Spiral stairs and stairs down from gardens and balconies
  • Cat ladders and walkways
  • Repairs and refurbishment of existing stairs

All work complies with building regulations.
